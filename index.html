The Referral Ceiling

Your clinic can only grow as fast as referrals come in.

Then, at some point, it stops getting better. It doesn't crash. It just stops. The same number of new patients, month after month. You look at the appointment book on a Monday morning and you can more or less guess the month already.

So you've got referrals you can't speed up, panels that set your fees, and a landlord who doesn't care about either. When you put that together, a lot of your month is decided by people who aren't you.

Rented marketing

Every month, for as long as you want it to work. And the moment you stop paying, what's left? Some posts on a page, a report full of views, and no system.

Questions

Questions you might have

Is this allowed? I don't want MAB trouble.
Every piece is checked against MAB LIU 1/2026, MMC and (for dentists) MDC rules before it goes live. We start with teaching content, which doesn't need approval. Ads go out only once MAB approves them.
I don't have time.
Your time goes into a couple of Brain interviews and a few short reviews. After that the AI team and front desk do the work.
I tried an agency. Lots of posts, no patients.
Agencies rent you their work, and when you stop paying it stops. This is built on your own Brain, and you own it once it's built.
What happens if I stop paying you?
Nothing stops. The build is one time. You only pay your own AI subscriptions, about RM130 a month for a single user.
Will the AI say something wrong to a patient?
It answers from your clinic's own data, not from the internet. It never gives clinical advice, outcome claims or patient information. It hands over to your staff when it doesn't know.
I'm a dentist. Same rules?
Dentists also follow the MDC Code and the Dental Act 2018, and dental promotions get extra scrutiny. We build that in.
